01 What we do

Interior renovation.

Room by room, or the whole floor, including flooring and painting.

The work

Interior renovation is the umbrella for the work that changes how the inside of a house reads: flooring run through connected rooms, paint, trim and doors, walls opened up or moved, and the small carpentry that ties it together.

On this kind of job the sequencing matters more than any single trade, because you are usually living in the house while it happens. The order of rooms is planned so the house keeps working around the job, and the site is cleaned before we leave it, every day it runs.

Two custom homes have been built under ZEF Design Build as well.

The quote

Scope

One room or the whole floor

A single room is a contained job. A whole floor is a sequence of them, and the quote reads room by room so you can see where it goes.

Surfaces

Flooring and paint

The two trades that carry most of the visual change, and most of the material choice. Continuous flooring through connected rooms is what makes a floor read as one space.

Structure

Whether walls move

Opening or moving a wall changes the job from surfaces to structure. It is drawn first, and where the city needs to see it, the drawing goes in for a permit.

Estimates are free, at your property. If anything behind the walls could change the number, you hear about it the day it is found.
